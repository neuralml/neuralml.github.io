In an experimental-theory collaboration with Mark Wagner's lab at NIH, we have tested some of the ideas that we have previously proposed (Pemberton et al. 2024): that the cerebellum contextualises cortical dynamics thus enabling continual learning of tasks. The paper is now out in [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10946-1).
